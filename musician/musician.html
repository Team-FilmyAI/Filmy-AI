About Me

My name is Arijit Singh, and I am an Indian playback singer, known for my soulful and versatile voice. I was born on April 25, 1987, in Jiaganj, Murshidabad, West Bengal, India. My journey into music began at an early age, and I honed my skills in classical music, which laid the foundation for my singing career.

I gained fame after participating in the reality TV show Fame Gurukul in 2005, though it was my work as a playback singer in Bollywood films that truly made me a household name. One of my biggest breakthroughs came with the song Tum Hi Ho from the movie Aashiqui 2 (2013), which earned me widespread recognition. Since then, I've sung a variety of hits across different genres, including romantic ballads, peppy tracks, and emotional numbers.

Some of my popular songs include Channa Mereya, Raabta, Agar Tum Saath Ho, Ilahi, and Phir Le Aaya Dil. My voice is often praised for its depth, emotion, and the way I connect with the listeners.

I’ve also won numerous awards, including Filmfare and IIFA Awards, which reflect the love and appreciation I have received from both fans and critics alike. Beyond playback singing, I occasionally compose and produce music, which allows me to express my creativity in new ways.

Despite the fame, I prefer a relatively private life, focusing more on my art and family. Music is my true passion, and I consider myself fortunate to be able to touch people's hearts through it.
